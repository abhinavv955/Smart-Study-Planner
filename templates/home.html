What is Smart Study Planner & Task Scheduler?

Smart Study Planner & Task Scheduler is a smart academic platform designed to help students plan their study schedules, track daily learning activities, and evaluate performance through subject-wise weekly tests and visual analytics.

Key Features

📅

Smart Study Planner

Automatically generate optimized study schedules based on subject difficulty, available study hours, and upcoming exam timelines. This helps students plan their preparation efficiently.

📘

Study Log

Record daily study sessions and keep track of completed learning activities. The system monitors consistency and provides insights into study habits.

📝

Weekly Tests

Evaluate subject understanding through automated MCQ-based assessments. Weekly quizzes help reinforce concepts and measure learning progress.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Visualize study performance using interactive charts, progress indicators, and summarized reports that help identify strengths and weak areas.
